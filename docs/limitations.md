# Scientific limitations

This platform does not establish causal effects, city-wide representative exposure from sparse stations, live hospital capacity from facility locations, or a universal resilience score. Routing is conditional on supplied topology and travel-time assumptions. Climate model layers are not current meteorological measurements. Energy candidate selection uses a chronological holdout; selection on that holdout means the selected error is not an independent final test estimate. A publication-quality evaluation needs another untouched test period or nested temporal evaluation.

No uncertainty interval is emitted without calibration. Historical next-step energy artifacts may be stale when created from older data, and are not used as current scenario baselines. Domain availability is not a statement that every record is fresh or scientifically valid. Review per-source timestamps and quality notes.
